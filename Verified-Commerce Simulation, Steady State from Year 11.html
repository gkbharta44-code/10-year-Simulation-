Verified-Commerce 10-Year Simulation

Years 1 to 5 follow the India pilot: 7.5 million vendors and 7.5 million orders in total. Years 6 to 10 grow vendors from 7.5 million to 3 billion, and orders per vendor per month rise to 1 by year 10, so orders reach 3 billion a month. Renewal opt-outs and seller economics are included. From year 11, vendors hold at the year-10 target. Each year, 10% of vendors opt out at renewal and new vendors replace them, so turnover stays constant.

Inputs

Orders per vendor per month ramp geometrically from the year-5 pilot rate to the year-10 target. Vendors grow geometrically from 7.5M to the year-10 target. Renewal opt-outs apply at the start of each year. Seller net is the order value less the lead fee, commission, and prepayment, minus each vendor's subscription and QC fees paid to SGS. QC fees are a pass-through and do not add to platform revenue.
